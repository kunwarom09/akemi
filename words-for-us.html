  • Reach a Broader Audience — Connect with readers who are actively interested in wellness support products, healthy habits, daily consistency, and lifestyle improvement.
  • Build Your Personal Brand — Gain exposure as a contributor while building your online presence and credibility in the wellness and lifestyle space.
  • Educate & Inspire Readers — Help readers make more informed lifestyle and wellness decisions through useful, practical, and responsible content.

Submission Guidelines

To maintain quality and relevance for our audience, please follow these submission guidelines carefully:

📝 Content Length

Articles should generally be 750+ words.

🔄 Original Content Only

We only accept original content that has not been published elsewhere. Submissions may be reviewed for plagiarism and duplicate content.

📚 Informational Focus

We prioritize educational and reader-focused content rather than aggressive promotional material or direct sales pitches.

⭐ Content Quality

Submissions should be:

  • Clear and easy to read
  • Well-structured
  • Informative and practical
  • Free from misleading health claims
  • Written in natural English

✏️ Editing Rights

We reserve the right to edit submitted content for:

  • Grammar
  • Clarity
  • Formatting
  • Readability
  • Redundancy
  • Style consistency

🖼️ Media & Permissions

Contributors are responsible for ensuring they have the rights or permissions to use any images, graphics, videos, or external media included with submissions.

👤 Author Bio

Please include a short author bio (2–3 sentences) along with:

  • Your name
  • Relevant expertise or background
  • Website or social media links (optional)

Important Content Standards

Because our website discusses wellness-related topics, contributors must avoid:

  • Unrealistic medical claims
  • Guaranteed weight-loss promises
  • Misleading supplement claims
  • False health information
  • Harmful or unsafe advice

We value responsible wellness communication and realistic expectations.
