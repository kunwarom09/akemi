How to Use

Simple Enough to
Actually Stick With

For best use, apply Akemi Slim Patch to clean, dry skin. Press firmly so the patch sticks well. Avoid applying it over irritated skin, cuts, lotion, or heavy sweat.

01

Clean & Dry

Clean and dry the target skin area. Remove all lotions, oils, and moisture. The midsection or abdominal area is popular for comfort and discretion.

02

Peel the Layer

Peel off the protective backing layer from the patch. Handle it by the edges to keep the adhesive surface clean before application.

03

Apply Firmly

Press the patch firmly onto your skin and hold for a few seconds. Smooth from center to edges to ensure full adhesion and a secure hold.

04

Replace Daily

Wear as directed and replace with a fresh patch each day. Rotate your application spots regularly to avoid skin irritation over time.
